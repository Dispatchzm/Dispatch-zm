DISPATCH WEBSITE

How to use
1. Unzip and upload everything inside this folder to your hosting, keeping all files together in one folder.
2. Open index.html. That is the home page.

Pages
index.html - home page
login.html, signup.html - account screens (screens only, no backend)
load-dispatch.html - Local / Outside loads chooser
local-loads.html - 12 local loads
outside-loads.html - 8 outside loads
(Details and Confirm open on the same page when you tap the icon or Confirm button)
truck-sourcing.html - truck categories, with List your truck button
list-your-truck.html - truck listing form
30-tonne-trucks.html, refrigerator-trucks.html, super-link-trucks.html, no-trailer-trucks.html, below-30-tonnes.html, work-construction-trucks.html, trucks-for-sale.html - truck category pages
old-landing.html - an older landing page, not linked from anywhere
